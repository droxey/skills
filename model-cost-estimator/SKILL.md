# Model Cost Estimator

## Maturity

Level 0 - Intent. Substantiated by the written contract only; no runnable asset or tests yet.

## Purpose

Estimate model usage cost from token and pricing assumptions.

## Inputs

Token assumptions, model pricing, and the scenario (or scenario comparison) to price.

## Outputs

A cost projection or comparison with the assumptions shown.

## Example

Estimate monthly cost for a traffic estimate at a given model's per-token price and note assumptions.

## Success criteria

Every estimate states its token, pricing, and rate assumptions explicitly.
